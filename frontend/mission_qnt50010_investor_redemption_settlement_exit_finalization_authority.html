QNT50010 — Investor Redemption Settlement + Exit Finalization Authority

Quantora now enforces a final institutional control layer after investor cash release. Executed treasury transfers, release authority lineage, reconciliation clearance, investor and operations attestation, and final exit authorization must all converge before an investor redemption can be marked fully closed.

Redemption close authority Treasury execution lineage Reconciliation clearance Audit-grade investor exit closure

Closure Discipline

  • Only executed investor redemption or capital-return transfers can become exit cases.
  • Investor release authority from QNT50009 is inherited into the exit finalization record.
  • Settlement breaks and incomplete attestations block final close authority.

Control Flow

  • register-case binds a completed treasury transfer to an investor exit case.
  • attest captures operations, investor, reconciliation, and legal evidence.
  • authorize-finalization issues expiring close authority.
  • finalize seals the investor exit as institutionally closed.
