QNT30692 — Audit & Regulatory System

Institutional audit and regulatory oversight layer for capital routing, allocation, disclosure, and governance readiness.

Core functions

  • Score audit viability using routing, allocation, orchestration, portfolio intelligence, governance, compliance, transparency, statements, and ledger completeness.
  • Determine whether the system should audit, remediate, disclose, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/audit-regulatory-system/summary
  • POST /api/audit-regulatory-system/run
  • GET /api/audit-regulatory-system/audit
  • POST /api/audit-regulatory-system/policy
