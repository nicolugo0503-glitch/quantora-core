"We don't predict markets. We engineer positions that profit regardless of direction — and cut losses before they compound."

Quantora Capital was built on a single conviction: that persistent, uncorrelated alpha is not found in consensus — it is extracted from structural inefficiencies that fundamental investors cannot access at speed, and that passive flows continuously recreate.

Our research infrastructure generates over 3,200 daily signals across 47 markets. The portfolio construction engine enforces strict correlation constraints, ensuring no two positions compound the same risk factor. The result is a fund that moves through market regimes — bull, bear, and sideways — without the cyclical dependence that defines 96% of institutional strategies.

We are soft-closed. We accept capital from endowments, family offices, and sovereign wealth funds on a selective basis, subject to capacity constraints and alignment of investment horizon. We do not take retail capital, and we do not participate in performance league tables.
