Investment Strategy

Systematic Alpha
Across Asset Classes

Quantora deploys a rigorously tested ensemble of quantitative models across eight distinct strategy clusters, operating simultaneously across 2,847 instruments in 52 global markets.

Signal Generation. The platform processes over 847 billion discrete data signals daily, spanning alternative data, order flow microstructure, cross-asset momentum, fundamental factor models, and macroeconomic regime classification. Each signal is individually tested for out-of-sample predictive validity before inclusion in the ensemble.

Portfolio Construction. Position sizing employs a proprietary risk-parity methodology with real-time regime adjustment. The optimizer targets a Sharpe ratio of 3.5 or above at the portfolio level, with maximum drawdown constraints applied at both the strategy and aggregate portfolio level. No single position may represent more than 2.4% of NAV.

Execution Architecture. Order execution is managed through proprietary smart-order-routing infrastructure with co-located servers at the major global exchanges. Average execution latency of 0.3 milliseconds ensures minimal market impact, with transaction cost analysis consistently showing costs below 0.4 basis points per trade.

Risk Management. An independent risk committee reviews all strategy parameters monthly. Real-time risk monitoring operates on a 24-hour basis with automatic circuit-breakers for anomalous market conditions. The fund has not had a monthly drawdown exceeding 1.2% since inception.

Architecture Overview

Quantora's proprietary trading infrastructure is built on a microservices architecture processing market data from over 147 global venues simultaneously. The system is co-located in Equinix Tier IV data centres in New York, London, and Tokyo, providing deterministic sub-millisecond execution with full geographic redundancy.

The machine learning pipeline employs an ensemble of gradient-boosted trees, transformer-based sequence models, and reinforcement learning agents, each specialised across a distinct market regime. Models are evaluated daily against out-of-sample performance with automatic deactivation thresholds to prevent overfitting. The risk management system operates independently with the authority to reduce or close any position without discretionary override.

All systems maintain hot-standby failover with sub-second switchover time. Disaster recovery infrastructure replicates all critical systems to a geographically separated facility, with full test exercised quarterly.
