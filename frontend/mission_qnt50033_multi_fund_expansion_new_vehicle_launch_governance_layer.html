QNT50033 — Multi-Fund Expansion + New Vehicle Launch Governance Layer

Governed launch layer for expanding Quantora into additional funds, vehicles, and sleeves with escalation evidence, treasury capacity, charter alignment, and audit-grade launch control.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes allocation escalation, risk, treasury, and institutional charter posture before launch decisions.
  • Registers new vehicle launch cases only after executed QNT50031 escalation evidence exists.
  • Approves governed vehicle launches with seed-capital and live-mode controls.
  • Executes new fund / SPV / vehicle launches into a governed registry path with full audit lineage.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /vehicle-launch/health
  • GET /vehicle-launch/summary
  • POST /vehicle-launch/register-case
  • POST /vehicle-launch/approve
  • POST /vehicle-launch/execute
  • POST /vehicle-launch/close-case
