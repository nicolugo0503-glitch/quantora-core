QNT30680 — Live Allocation Recovery Authority

Institutional recovery layer deciding whether impaired live capital should recover, rotate, reduce, exit, or escalate.

Core functions

  • Measure drawdown severity and loss persistence from real ledger, pnl, and execution artifacts.
  • Score recovery viability using continuity, remediation, governance, and compliance posture.
  • Emit governed actions: RECOVER, ROTATE, REDUCE, EXIT, ESCALATE.
  • Persist recovery runs and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/live-allocation-recovery-authority/summary
  • POST /api/live-allocation-recovery-authority/run
  • GET /api/live-allocation-recovery-authority/audit
  • POST /api/live-allocation-recovery-authority/policy
