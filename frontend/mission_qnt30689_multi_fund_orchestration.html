QNT30689 — Multi-Fund Orchestration

Institutional orchestration layer for distributing governed capital across multiple funds after portfolio-intelligence analysis.

Core functions

  • Score fund-level orchestration viability using portfolio intelligence, strategy selection, regime detection, deployment quality, reallocation quality, governance, and compliance.
  • Determine whether each fund should orchestrate, rebalance, redistribute, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/multi-fund-orchestration/summary
  • POST /api/multi-fund-orchestration/run
  • GET /api/multi-fund-orchestration/audit
  • POST /api/multi-fund-orchestration/policy
