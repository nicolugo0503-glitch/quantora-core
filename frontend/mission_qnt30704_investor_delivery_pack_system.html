Statement Bridge

The system composes its output on top of the underlying statement-pack engine, so investor delivery inherits fund-level performance, waterfall, and PnL context.

Governance Bridge

Safety posture and operator context are embedded into the pack so outward-facing reporting reflects the live control environment of the capital system.

Distribution Memory

Delivery events, acknowledgement state, and recipient routing are stored for future reporting cycles and investor-coverage follow-up.
