QNT30682 — Drawdown Defense System

Institutional engine for defending live capital under drawdown pressure using recovery, restoration, continuity, governance, and compliance inputs.

Core functions

  • Score drawdown severity, persistence, volatility, recovery probability, and governance pressure.
  • Determine whether capital should be defended, reduced, hedged, held, or escalated.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/drawdown-defense-system/summary
  • POST /api/drawdown-defense-system/run
  • GET /api/drawdown-defense-system/audit
  • POST /api/drawdown-defense-system/policy
