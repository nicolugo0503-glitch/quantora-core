QNT50031 — Institutional Live Allocation Escalation + Capacity Ceiling Governance Layer

Governed escalation layer for raising strategy allocation beyond initial scale-up while enforcing hard capacity ceilings, risk clearance, treasury headroom, and institutional mandate discipline.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes live scale-up, risk, treasury, performance, and charter posture before any escalation action.
  • Registers escalation cases tied to executed QNT50030 ramp evidence.
  • Approves allocation escalation only within configured ceiling limits.
  • Executes governed escalation events into allocation and execution pathways with audit-grade traceability.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /allocation-escalation/health
  • GET /allocation-escalation/summary
  • POST /allocation-escalation/register-case
  • POST /allocation-escalation/approve
  • POST /allocation-escalation/execute
  • POST /allocation-escalation/close-case
