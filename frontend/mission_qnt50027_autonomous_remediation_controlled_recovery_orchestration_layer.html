QNT50027 — Autonomous Remediation + Controlled Recovery Orchestration Layer

Controlled institutional recovery layer for translating governed breach cases into remediation actions, autonomous recovery cycles, and supervised closure.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes breach, risk, treasury, and autonomous control posture before remediation.
  • Registers remediation actions with case lineage, capital-at-risk, and recovery intent.
  • Authorizes recovery only after governed breach resolution and supervisory prerequisites.
  • Executes controlled recovery cycles and closes actions with audit-grade traceability.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /autonomous-remediation/health
  • GET /autonomous-remediation/summary
  • POST /autonomous-remediation/register-action
  • POST /autonomous-remediation/authorize-recovery
  • POST /autonomous-remediation/execute-recovery
  • POST /autonomous-remediation/close-action
