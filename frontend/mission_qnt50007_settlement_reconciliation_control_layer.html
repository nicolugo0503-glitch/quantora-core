QNT50007 — Settlement + Reconciliation Control Layer

Quantora now extends beyond autonomous order routing into post-trade control. This layer ingests executed fills from QNT50001 and QNT50006, creates settlement tickets, confirms cash and custody movements, books internal ledgers, and runs broker-vs-internal reconciliation with explicit break detection.

Execution fill ingestion Settlement confirmation Cash and position ledgers Reconciliation break governance

Control Behavior

  • Executed fills are converted into settlement tickets with decision, allocation, strategy, and broker lineage.
  • Confirmed settlements update cash and position ledgers instead of leaving execution as an unclosed event.
  • Exceptions remain pending and are visible as unresolved post-trade control items.

Reconciliation

  • Internal cash and positions are compared against broker snapshots using institutional tolerances.
  • Breaks are classified and written into the reconciliation register for follow-up and remediation.
  • The latest reconciliation status is surfaced through a dedicated health endpoint.

Endpoints

  • GET /settlement/health
  • POST /settlement/ingest-fills
  • POST /settlement/confirm
  • POST /settlement/reconcile
  • GET /settlement/breaks
