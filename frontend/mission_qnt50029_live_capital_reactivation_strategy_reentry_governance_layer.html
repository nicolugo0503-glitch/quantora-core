QNT50029 — Live Capital Reactivation + Strategy Re-Entry Governance Layer

Governed strategy re-entry layer for reactivating capital only after post-recovery reinstatement, risk clearance, treasury capacity checks, and strategy profile validation.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes post-recovery reinstatement, strategy deployment, treasury, and risk posture before re-entry.
  • Registers strategy reactivation cases linked to executed reauthorization evidence.
  • Approves re-entry in paper or live mode subject to safe-mode and policy constraints.
  • Executes capital release into the execution queue with audit-grade traceability.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /strategy-reentry/health
  • GET /strategy-reentry/summary
  • POST /strategy-reentry/register-reactivation
  • POST /strategy-reentry/approve-reentry
  • POST /strategy-reentry/execute-reentry
  • POST /strategy-reentry/close-reactivation
