QNT30687 — Strategy Selection AI

Institutional intelligence layer for selecting, scaling, defending, or rebalancing strategies after regime detection.

Core functions

  • Score strategy-selection viability using regime posture, deployment quality, reallocation quality, rotation, defense, governance, compliance, and execution activity.
  • Determine whether the system should select, scale, defend, rebalance, review, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/strategy-selection-ai/summary
  • POST /api/strategy-selection-ai/run
  • GET /api/strategy-selection-ai/audit
  • POST /api/strategy-selection-ai/policy
