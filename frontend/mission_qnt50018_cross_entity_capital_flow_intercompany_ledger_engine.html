QNT50018 — Cross-Entity Capital Flow + Intercompany Ledger Engine

Institutional intercompany capital control layer for entity-to-entity cash movement, approval governance, dual-entry posting, and settlement lineage.

Capabilities

  • Registers cross-entity capital flows with fund, SPV, strategy, jurisdiction, and legal-entity metadata.
  • Uses treasury mobility context from QNT50008 and settlement posture from QNT50007.
  • Creates dual-entry journal records for affiliate receivable/payable positions.
  • Preserves approval, posting, and settlement lineage for audit-grade intercompany traceability.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /intercompany-ledger/health
  • GET /intercompany-ledger/summary
  • POST /intercompany-ledger/register-flow
  • POST /intercompany-ledger/approve
  • POST /intercompany-ledger/post
  • POST /intercompany-ledger/settle
