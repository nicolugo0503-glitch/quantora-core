QNT50005 — Performance Engine (Institutional)

Quantora now computes investor-grade performance intelligence across NAV history, drawdown, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, and strategy attribution. This layer turns execution activity into an auditable capital performance narrative instead of a raw trade log.

Sharpe + Sortino Drawdown intelligence Investor metrics Risk sync

Performance Logic

  • NAV snapshots are normalized into a return series with annualized return and volatility.
  • Max drawdown, current drawdown, best/worst day, and win rate are computed from the equity curve.
  • Strategy attribution aggregates pnl and contribution by strategy for institutional review.

Governance

  • Every recompute and snapshot registration is written into a dedicated audit ledger.
  • Latest drawdown and daily loss metrics can be synchronized directly into the QNT50004 risk layer.
  • Investor metrics expose MTD, QTD, YTD, and inception return from the official NAV path.
