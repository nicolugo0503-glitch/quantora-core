This mission institutionalizes investor-admission control across AML clearance, accreditation verification, suitability assessment, and final admission approval.

Institutional Purpose

Quantora now governs who can be admitted into capital structure, under what evidence standard, and with what compliance posture. This layer binds onboarding, identity, subscription, AML review, and final fund admission into one regulator-defensible control plane.
