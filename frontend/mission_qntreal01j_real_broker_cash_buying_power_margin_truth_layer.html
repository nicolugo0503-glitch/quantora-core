QNT-REAL01J — Real Broker Cash / Buying Power / Margin Truth Layer

This mission adds governed truth for cash balance, settled cash, unsettled cash, buying power, available buying power, initial margin used, maintenance margin used, and margin excess.

API endpoints:

  • GET /cash-truth/health
  • GET /cash-truth/summary
  • POST /cash-truth/sync-context
  • POST /cash-truth/recompute
  • POST /cash-truth/reset
