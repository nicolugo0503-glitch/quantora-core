QNT50030 — Live Strategy Scale-Up + Dynamic Capital Ramp Governance Layer

Governed scale-up layer for increasing live strategy capital only after controlled re-entry, risk clearance, treasury capacity checks, and ramp-policy enforcement.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes live re-entry, risk, treasury, strategy deployment, and performance posture before ramp-up.
  • Registers scale cases linked to executed QNT50029 re-entry evidence.
  • Approves dynamic ramp plans in paper or live mode subject to safe-mode and policy constraints.
  • Executes governed capital ramp events into allocation or execution pathways with audit-grade traceability.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /strategy-scale-up/health
  • GET /strategy-scale-up/summary
  • POST /strategy-scale-up/register-scale-case
  • POST /strategy-scale-up/approve-ramp
  • POST /strategy-scale-up/execute-ramp
  • POST /strategy-scale-up/close-scale-case
