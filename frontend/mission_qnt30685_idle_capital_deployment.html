QNT30685 — Idle Capital Deployment

Institutional engine for deploying idle capital into governed live allocations after strategy reallocation.

Core functions

  • Score deployment viability using reallocation, rotation, defense, restoration, continuity, governance, compliance, and idle-capital ratio.
  • Determine whether idle capital should be deployed, accelerated, rebalanced, held, or escalated.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/idle-capital-deployment/summary
  • POST /api/idle-capital-deployment/run
  • GET /api/idle-capital-deployment/audit
  • POST /api/idle-capital-deployment/policy
