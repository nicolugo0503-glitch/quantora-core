QNT50028 — Institutional Reauthorization + Post-Recovery Capital Reinstatement Layer

Governed post-recovery capital reinstatement layer for restoring capital only after recovery execution, risk clearance, and institutional reauthorization.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes remediation recovery, breach resolution, risk, and treasury posture before reauthorization.
  • Registers reauthorization cases with recovery lineage, requested capital, and target strategy context.
  • Approves reinstatement only when risk is clear, recovery is evidenced, and treasury capacity exists.
  • Executes and closes reinstatement with audit-grade traceability tied to the linked recovery action.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /capital-reauthorization/health
  • GET /capital-reauthorization/summary
  • POST /capital-reauthorization/register-reauthorization
  • POST /capital-reauthorization/approve-reinstatement
  • POST /capital-reauthorization/execute-reinstatement
  • POST /capital-reauthorization/close-reauthorization
