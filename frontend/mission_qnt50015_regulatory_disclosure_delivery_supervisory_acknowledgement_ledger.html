QNT50015 — Regulatory Disclosure Delivery + Supervisory Acknowledgement Ledger

Institutional disclosure control layer for delivery of published governance binders and audit-grade supervisory acknowledgement capture.

Capabilities

  • Registers disclosure delivery only against real QNT50014 published binder evidence.
  • Records delivery receipts with channel and reference lineage.
  • Maintains supervisory acknowledgement ledger with outcome tracking and exception capture.
  • Preserves period, books release, official release, retrieval packet, and binder publication lineage.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /regulatory-disclosures/health
  • GET /regulatory-disclosures/summary
  • POST /regulatory-disclosures/register-delivery
  • POST /regulatory-disclosures/record-receipt
  • POST /regulatory-disclosures/acknowledge
