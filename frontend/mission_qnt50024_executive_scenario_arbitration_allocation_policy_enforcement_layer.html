QNT50024 — Executive Scenario Arbitration + Allocation Policy Enforcement Layer

Institutional executive layer that arbitrates capital scenarios against committee context, liquidity posture, and allocation policy, then issues governed directives for downstream execution and treasury systems.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes committee, autonomous control, risk, treasury, performance, deployment, and intercompany posture.
  • Registers allocation policies with allowed and blocked action scopes, thresholds, and entity context.
  • Arbitrates proposed capital changes using resilience, policy alignment, downside, and liquidity coverage scoring.
  • Issues enforceable directives only when scenario decisions clear policy and institutional control thresholds.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /executive-arbitration/health
  • GET /executive-arbitration/summary
  • POST /executive-arbitration/register-policy
  • POST /executive-arbitration/arbitrate
  • POST /executive-arbitration/enforce-policy
  • POST /executive-arbitration/reset
