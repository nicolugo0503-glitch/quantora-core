Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a free tier?
Quantora does not offer a free tier — we are a serious professional platform, not a trial app. All plans start at $79/month. This keeps the community focused and serious.
Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade immediately — access unlocks the moment payment is confirmed. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
What does "governed live execution" mean?
Quantora Execution adds pre-trade checks, buying power enforcement, order-risk rules, and post-trade reconciliation — so your capital is protected by a structured operating framework, not just your judgment alone.
What's included in Firm onboarding?
Quantora Firm includes a structured onboarding process: workflow mapping, governance configuration, supervisory setup, and operator certification. Optional deeper implementation packages are available.
Is paper trading just a demo?
Quantora's paper trading system is a serious posture tool — not a toy. It runs on live market data with real risk metrics, P&L tracking, and capital discipline. It is how you prove readiness before live deployment.
What about Institutional pricing?
Institutional engagements are custom and start at $10,000/month. They include private deployment options, dedicated implementation, and full governance customization. Contact us to begin the conversation.
