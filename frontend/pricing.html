Frequently Asked Questions
Is there really a free plan? ▼
Yes. The Starter plan is permanently free with no credit card required. You get real-time quotes, 50 intelligence panels, and $10K paper trading capital right away.
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. No cancellation fees, no lock-ins. If you cancel a paid plan, you keep access until the end of your billing period.
What brokers does Institutional support? ▼
We support Binance (crypto), Interactive Brokers (IBKR) for equities/futures/options, and Alpaca for commission-free US equities. More brokers are being added continuously.
Is the paper trading connected to real market prices? ▼
Yes. Paper trading uses live mark-to-market pricing from real-time Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko feeds. Your P&L, risk metrics, and portfolio values reflect actual current prices.
Can I use the API to build my own tools? ▼
Absolutely. All plans include API access. The Pro and Institutional plans include interactive Swagger UI documentation at /docs with 50+ endpoints covering market data, portfolio, execution, and analytics.
Is Quantora financial advice? ▼
No. Quantora is a data and intelligence platform. Nothing on Quantora constitutes financial, investment, or trading advice. All trading involves risk. Paper trading is for educational and research purposes only.
