QNT50035 — Multi-Region Operating Expansion + Jurisdictional Service Partition Layer

Governed regional expansion layer for partitioning shared services across jurisdictions with capacity, compliance, treasury, and audit-grade operating controls.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes shared-services execution evidence, risk posture, treasury capacity, and optional compliance posture before regional expansion registration.
  • Registers regional operating expansion cases with explicit jurisdictions, service partitions, and governed regional budget limits.
  • Approves and executes jurisdictional service partitions with safe-mode live blocking and treasury-capacity enforcement.
  • Records regional partition execution events with full audit lineage for future multi-jurisdiction operating governance.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /region-partition/health
  • GET /region-partition/summary
  • POST /region-partition/register-case
  • POST /region-partition/approve
  • POST /region-partition/execute
  • POST /region-partition/close-case
