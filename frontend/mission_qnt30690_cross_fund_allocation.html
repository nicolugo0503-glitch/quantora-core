QNT30690 — Cross-Fund Allocation

Institutional allocation layer for distributing governed capital across funds after multi-fund orchestration.

Core functions

  • Score cross-fund allocation viability using multi-fund orchestration, portfolio intelligence, strategy selection, regime detection, governance, and compliance.
  • Determine whether each fund should allocate, rebalance, redistribute, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/cross-fund-allocation/summary
  • POST /api/cross-fund-allocation/run
  • GET /api/cross-fund-allocation/audit
  • POST /api/cross-fund-allocation/policy
