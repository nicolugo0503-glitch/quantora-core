QNT30695 — Autonomous Fund Mode

Institutional control layer for autonomous operation of the fund after reporting, transparency, audit, routing, governance, and compliance readiness.

Core functions

  • Score autonomous-fund viability using reporting quality, transparency quality, audit quality, routing quality, governance posture, compliance posture, treasury readiness, mobility readiness, and runbook completeness.
  • Determine whether the fund should autonomize, review, require runbook completion, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/autonomous-fund-mode/summary
  • POST /api/autonomous-fund-mode/run
  • GET /api/autonomous-fund-mode/audit
  • POST /api/autonomous-fund-mode/policy
