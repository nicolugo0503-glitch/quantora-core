QNT50004 — Risk Kill-Switch System

Quantora now enforces a capital protection nerve center that can hard-stop execution when drawdown, daily loss, concentration, margin, or venue health moves outside institutional policy. This is not advisory. It is a mandatory control layer wired directly into the execution path.

Drawdown enforcement Live execution hard-stop Safe mode fallback Override ledger

Protection Logic

  • Portfolio drawdown, strategy drawdown, daily loss, notional, concentration, margin, and venue connectivity are evaluated continuously.
  • Critical breaches trigger an immediate kill-switch and force execution back into paper-safe mode.
  • Pre-trade checks block oversized orders before they can reach a broker.

Governance

  • Every trigger, blocked order, reset, and override is written into a dedicated audit trail.
  • Overrides require operator attribution and a control ticket.
  • The kill-switch synchronizes execution, allocation, and deployment layers into the same safety posture.
