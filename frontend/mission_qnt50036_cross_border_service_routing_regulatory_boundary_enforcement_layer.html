QNT50036 — Cross-Border Service Routing + Regulatory Boundary Enforcement Layer

Governed cross-border routing layer for regional service flows, jurisdictional boundary controls, compliance-aware approvals, and audit-grade route execution evidence.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes regional partition evidence, risk posture, treasury capacity, and optional compliance decisions before cross-border routing registration.
  • Registers cross-border routing cases with source/destination jurisdictions, service channels, route limits, and boundary-policy lineage.
  • Approves and executes cross-border routing with live-mode safe-mode blocking and regulatory boundary clearance enforcement.
  • Records routing execution events with full audit lineage for future supervisory and cross-jurisdiction operations governance.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /cross-border-routing/health
  • GET /cross-border-routing/summary
  • POST /cross-border-routing/register-case
  • POST /cross-border-routing/approve
  • POST /cross-border-routing/execute
  • POST /cross-border-routing/close-case
