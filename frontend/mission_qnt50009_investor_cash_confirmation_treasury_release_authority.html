QNT50009 — Investor Cash Confirmation + Treasury Release Authority

Quantora now places an institutional release-authority gate between treasury approval and investor cash movement. Redemptions, distributions, and capital-return flows must pass investor registration, bank-instruction verification, dual acknowledgement, statement alignment, and treasury-capacity checks before treasury execution is allowed to proceed.

Investor cash release authority Dual acknowledgement workflow Treasury transfer binding Audit-grade release control

Release Discipline

  • Only investor-directed treasury transfers require the QNT50009 authority gate.
  • Bank instruction verification and statement alignment are recorded at the investor level.
  • Treasury execution is blocked until an active release authority exists for the transfer.

Control Flow

  • register-investor establishes confirmation posture.
  • request-release binds the investor to an approved treasury transfer.
  • acknowledge captures operations and investor confirmation evidence.
  • authorize-release issues expiring release authority for execution.
