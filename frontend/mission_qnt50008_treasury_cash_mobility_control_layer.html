QNT50008 — Treasury + Cash Mobility Control Layer

Quantora now governs post-settlement liquidity as institutional treasury, not just as execution infrastructure. This layer synchronizes with QNT50007 cash state, enforces reserve buffers, stages and approves treasury transfers, and plans internal rebalancing before cash is dispatched to brokers, custody, administrators, or investor settlement rails.

Liquidity reserve policy Transfer staging and approvals Cash mobility governance Internal treasury rebalancing

Control Behavior

  • Settlement cash and pending obligations are synced into treasury liquidity context before mobility decisions are made.
  • Reserve floors and operating cash targets are preserved before any transfer is released.
  • Transfers exceeding policy thresholds or active reconciliation breaks are forced into review instead of silent execution.

Treasury Accounts

  • operating maintains internal working cash.
  • broker_buffer supports venue funding and execution readiness.
  • custody_reserve protects institutional reserve capital.

Endpoints

  • GET /treasury/health
  • POST /treasury/sync-settlement
  • POST /treasury/stage-transfer
  • POST /treasury/approve-transfer
  • POST /treasury/execute-transfer
