QNT30683 — Capital Rotation Engine

Institutional engine for rotating defended or restored capital into stronger live allocations.

Core functions

  • Score rotation viability using drawdown defense, restoration, recovery, continuity, governance, compliance, pnl, and allocation concentration.
  • Determine whether capital should be rotated, rebalanced, reduced, held, or escalated.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/capital-rotation-engine/summary
  • POST /api/capital-rotation-engine/run
  • GET /api/capital-rotation-engine/audit
  • POST /api/capital-rotation-engine/policy
