Quantora • Period Close Distribution Governance

QNT50012 — Period Close Distribution Ledger + Investor Notice Finalization

Quantora now locks executed investor distributions into a period-close ledger, finalizes investor notice packets, and blocks close completion until both accounting and communications evidence are complete.

Period close ledger Investor notice finalization Close authority Audit-ready distribution evidence

Control Flow

  • register-close compiles executed payables for a reporting period.
  • finalize-ledger seals the accounting ledger once breaks and attestation checks pass.
  • finalize-notice finalizes each investor notice packet with channel and expiry metadata.
  • close-period locks the period only after ledger and notices are complete.

Institutional Checks

  • Open settlement breaks can block ledger finalization.
  • Ops and finance attestation can be required before close.
  • All investor notices must be finalized before period close can be sealed.

Endpoints

  • GET /period-close-distributions/health
  • POST /period-close-distributions/register-close
  • POST /period-close-distributions/finalize-ledger
  • POST /period-close-distributions/finalize-notice
  • POST /period-close-distributions/close-period
