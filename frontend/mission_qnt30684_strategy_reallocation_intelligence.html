QNT30684 — Strategy Reallocation Intelligence

Institutional intelligence layer for reallocating live capital across strategies after rotation and drawdown defense.

Core functions

  • Score reallocation viability using rotation, defense, restoration, recovery, continuity, governance, compliance, pnl, and allocation concentration.
  • Determine whether capital should be reallocated, rebalanced, reduced, held, or escalated.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/strategy-reallocation-intelligence/summary
  • POST /api/strategy-reallocation-intelligence/run
  • GET /api/strategy-reallocation-intelligence/audit
  • POST /api/strategy-reallocation-intelligence/policy
