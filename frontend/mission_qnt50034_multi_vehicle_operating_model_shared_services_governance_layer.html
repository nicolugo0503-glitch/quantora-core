QNT50034 — Multi-Vehicle Operating Model + Shared Services Governance Layer

Governed shared-services layer for operating multiple Quantora vehicles under common service functions, budget control, directive alignment, and audit-grade execution traceability.

Capabilities

  • Synchronizes vehicle launch evidence, risk posture, treasury posture, and institutional directives before shared-services registration.
  • Registers operating models for finance, operations, compliance, investor relations, and other shared-service functions.
  • Approves governed shared-services models with live-mode controls and budget approval gates.
  • Executes multi-vehicle service activation into a governed registry path with full audit lineage.

Primary endpoints

  • GET /shared-services/health
  • GET /shared-services/summary
  • POST /shared-services/register-model
  • POST /shared-services/approve
  • POST /shared-services/execute
  • POST /shared-services/close-model
