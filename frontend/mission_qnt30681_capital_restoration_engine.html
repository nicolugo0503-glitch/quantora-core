QNT30681 — Capital Restoration Engine

Institutional engine for restoring damaged but recoverable capital after recovery authority review.

Core functions

  • Score restoration viability using recovery, remediation, continuity, governance, compliance, pnl, and ledger artifacts.
  • Determine whether capital should be restored, reviewed, held, or escalated.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/capital-restoration-engine/summary
  • POST /api/capital-restoration-engine/run
  • GET /api/capital-restoration-engine/audit
  • POST /api/capital-restoration-engine/policy
