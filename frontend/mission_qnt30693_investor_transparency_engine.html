QNT30693 — Investor Transparency Engine

Institutional transparency layer for investor-facing disclosure, statement coverage, and trust posture after audit/regulatory review.

Core functions

  • Score investor transparency using audit quality, routing quality, compliance posture, disclosure completeness, statement coverage, and ledger/pnl coverage.
  • Determine whether the system should publish, remediate, disclose, issue statements, hold, or escalate.
  • Persist run history and policy controls for auditability.

Endpoints

  • GET /api/investor-transparency-engine/summary
  • POST /api/investor-transparency-engine/run
  • GET /api/investor-transparency-engine/audit
  • POST /api/investor-transparency-engine/policy
