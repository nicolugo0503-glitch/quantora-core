Quantora • Investor Distribution Governance

QNT50011 — Investor Distribution Waterfall + Payable Release Authority

Quantora now computes investor distribution waterfalls, binds each payable to treasury transfers, and enforces transfer-specific payable authority before cash leaves the institution. Distribution batches inherit performance, treasury, and investor registry context before release.

Waterfall allocation Batch authority Transfer binding Investor payable release gate

Control Flow

  • register-batch computes a payable waterfall across registered investors.
  • attest captures operations, finance, and reporting evidence.
  • authorize-batch approves the distribution envelope against treasury and performance constraints.
  • bind-transfer maps treasury transfers to investor lines.
  • authorize-payable issues transfer-specific release authority.

Institutional Checks

  • Treasury capacity is checked before payable authority is granted.
  • Settlement and reconciliation breaks can block distributions.
  • Only approved distribution transfers can be released for execution.

Endpoints

  • GET /investor-distributions/health
  • POST /investor-distributions/register-batch
  • POST /investor-distributions/authorize-batch
  • POST /investor-distributions/bind-transfer
  • POST /investor-distributions/authorize-payable
