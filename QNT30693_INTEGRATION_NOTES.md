# QNT30693 Integration Notes

Mission: QNT30693 Investor Transparency Engine

This layer consumes audit & regulatory system outputs, global capital routing, cross-fund allocation,
institutional compliance, transparency-layer signals, investor statements, pnl, and capital-ledger
artifacts to determine the investor-facing transparency posture of the institution.

Primary outputs:
- investor transparency overview
- transparency book
- transparency decisions
- transparency agenda
- audit history

Institutional actions:
- PUBLISH
- REMEDIATE
- DISCLOSE
- STATEMENT
- HOLD
- ESCALATE
