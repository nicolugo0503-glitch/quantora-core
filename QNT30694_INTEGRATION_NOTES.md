# QNT30694 Integration Notes

Mission: QNT30694 Reporting + Disclosure Automation

This layer consumes investor transparency engine outputs, audit & regulatory system outputs,
global capital routing, institutional compliance, transparency-layer signals, investor statements,
identity, pnl, and capital-ledger artifacts to determine the reporting and disclosure automation
posture of the institution.

- reporting + disclosure overview
- reporting book
- reporting decisions
- reporting agenda
- audit history

Institutional actions:
- REPORT
- REMEDIATE
- DISCLOSE
- STATEMENT
- AUTOMATE
- HOLD
- ESCALATE
