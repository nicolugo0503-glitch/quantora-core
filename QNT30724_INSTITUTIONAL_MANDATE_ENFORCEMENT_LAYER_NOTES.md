# QNT30724 — Institutional Mandate Enforcement Layer

This mission adds a governed mandate enforcement layer that sits after execution charter approval.

It evaluates whether a proposed allocation remains inside institutional mandate constraints across:
- concentration
- control coverage
- jurisdiction alignment
- investor restriction alignment
- leverage posture

It also cross-checks release, safety, fund admin, forensic, recovery, liquidity, regime, defense, executive, committee, policy, and charter posture before allowing mandate enforcement to clear.
