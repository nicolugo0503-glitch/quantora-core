# QNT30761 — Regulatory Resolution Planning and Wind-Down Control Layer

## Institutional Purpose
Establish Quantora's regulator-grade resolution planning and orderly wind-down command layer.

## Integrated Control Domains
- multi-jurisdiction governance
- capital adequacy surveillance
- liquidity stress command and recovery
- breach escalation and remediation
- enforcement response and consent order command

## Added Control Workflows
- resolution plan registration
- critical function mapping
- wind-down readiness review
- resolution posture evaluation

## Control Objective
Ensure Quantora can evidence orderly recovery, transfer, or controlled wind-down without breaking capital integrity, governance continuity, or regulatory readiness.
