# QNT50007 — Settlement + Reconciliation Control Layer

This mission adds the institutional post-trade control plane.

## Scope
- Ingest fills generated by QNT50001 and QNT50006
- Create settlement tickets with decision lineage
- Confirm cash and custody completion
- Book cash and position ledgers
- Reconcile internal balances against broker snapshots
- Surface breaks for remediation and supervision

## Institutional posture
Execution is not treated as complete until settlement and reconciliation status are visible and governed.
