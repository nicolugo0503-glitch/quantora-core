# QNT50015 — Regulatory Disclosure Delivery + Supervisory Acknowledgement Ledger

This mission extends the institutional close chain after QNT50014.

## Mission intent
- deliver published governance binder disclosures through an auditable supervisory channel
- capture delivery receipt evidence before acknowledgement when policy requires it
- maintain acknowledgement ledger entries with lineage back to official books and period close artifacts

## Control posture
- requires a real published binder from QNT50014
- requires retrieval packet evidence when policy is enabled
- blocks acknowledgement without a delivery receipt when policy is enabled
- stores exceptions for non-accepted acknowledgement outcomes
