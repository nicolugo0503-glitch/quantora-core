# QNT30710 — Market Regime Intelligence System

Adds a governed market regime layer on top of the clean deploy base.

## Scope
- volatility spike monitoring
- trend breadth scoring
- cross-asset stress scoring
- macro alignment and dispersion posture
- liquidity, safety, recovery, forensic, and strategy cross-checks

## Result
- regime cycles can be evaluated and stored
- blocked or watch regimes raise alerts
- favorable regimes are recorded for operator supervision
