# QNT30472 — Treasury, Cash Management, and Yield Optimization Layer

## Mission objective
Install the treasury layer across accounts, cash movements, yield strategies, and liquidity snapshots.

## Added backend capabilities
- treasury account registry
- cash movement registry
- yield strategy registry
- liquidity snapshot registry
- treasury summary package and health score

## Added frontend capability
- treasury and yield optimization panel

## Key endpoints
- `GET /workspace/treasury/summary`
- `POST /workspace/treasury/accounts/create`
- `POST /workspace/treasury/movements/create`
- `POST /workspace/treasury/yield/create`
- `POST /workspace/treasury/liquidity/create`

## Strategic value
This mission gives Quantora institutional treasury infrastructure for cash control and yield optimization.
