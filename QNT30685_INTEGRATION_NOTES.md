# QNT30685 Integration Notes

Mission: QNT30685 Idle Capital Deployment

This layer consumes strategy reallocation, capital rotation, drawdown defense, restoration,
continuity, governance, committee oversight, compliance, pnl, and capital-ledger artifacts
to determine how idle capital should be deployed.

Primary outputs:
- idle capital deployment overview
- deployment book
- deployment decisions
- deployment agenda
- audit history

Institutional actions:
- DEPLOY
- ACCELERATE
- REBALANCE
- HOLD
- ESCALATE
