# QNT30684 Integration Notes

Mission: QNT30684 Strategy Reallocation Intelligence

This layer consumes capital rotation, drawdown defense, restoration, recovery, continuity,
governance, committee oversight, compliance, pnl, and capital-ledger artifacts to determine
how live capital should be reallocated across strategies.

Primary outputs:
- strategy reallocation overview
- reallocation book
- reallocation decisions
- reallocation agenda
- audit history

Institutional actions:
- REALLOCATE
- REBALANCE
- REDUCE
- HOLD
- ESCALATE
