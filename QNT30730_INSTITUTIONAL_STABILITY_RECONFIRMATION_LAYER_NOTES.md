# QNT30730 — Institutional Stability Reconfirmation Layer

This mission governs formal reconfirmation that Quantora has returned to a stable institutional operating posture after continuity restoration.

## Scope
- score reconfirmation quality across monitoring breaches, reconciliation breaks, complaints, service degradation, and reporting timeliness
- cross-check operator, release, safety, forensic, recovery, reauthorization, breach, exception, remediation closure, and continuity posture
- produce a governed reconfirmation score, stability band, and posture
- record stability-book entries and alerts
