# QNT30473 — Revenue Operations + Billing Intelligence Layer

## Mission objective
Install the revenue layer across billing accounts, invoices, subscriptions, and collections logs.

## Added backend capabilities
- billing account registry
- invoice registry
- subscription registry
- collections registry
- revenue operations summary package and health score

## Added frontend capability
- revenue operations and billing intelligence panel

## Key endpoints
- `GET /workspace/revenue-ops/summary`
- `POST /workspace/revenue-ops/accounts/create`
- `POST /workspace/revenue-ops/invoices/create`
- `POST /workspace/revenue-ops/subscriptions/create`
- `POST /workspace/revenue-ops/collections/create`

## Strategic value
This mission gives Quantora the operating layer for measurable recurring revenue and collections performance.
