# QNT30652 Institutional Compliance Layer

Institutional supervisory layer merged into the master Quantora system.

## Added
- Institutional compliance router with summary, run, audit, and policy endpoints.
- Compliance readiness scoring across KYC, audit chain, governance backlog, growth release, and allocator transparency.
- Mission page for supervised release status.
- Manifest and mission registry updates.

## Endpoints
- GET /api/institutional-compliance-layer/summary
- POST /api/institutional-compliance-layer/run
- GET /api/institutional-compliance-layer/audit
- POST /api/institutional-compliance-layer/policy
