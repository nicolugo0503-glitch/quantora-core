# QNT30474 — Investor CRM + Relationship Intelligence Layer

## Mission objective
Install the investor relationship layer across profiles, stages, touchpoints, and intelligence notes.

## Added backend capabilities
- investor profile registry
- relationship stage registry
- touchpoint registry
- intelligence note registry
- investor CRM summary package and health score

## Added frontend capability
- investor CRM and relationship intelligence panel

## Key endpoints
- `GET /workspace/investor-crm/summary`
- `POST /workspace/investor-crm/profiles/create`
- `POST /workspace/investor-crm/stages/create`
- `POST /workspace/investor-crm/touchpoints/create`
- `POST /workspace/investor-crm/notes/create`

## Strategic value
This mission gives Quantora a structured allocator relationship engine that compounds fundraising intelligence.
