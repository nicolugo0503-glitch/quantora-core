"Edge is not a strategy. Edge is the persistent statistical residual after every rational participant has traded against you — and alpha remains."

Quantora operates on a simple but rigorous premise: structured inefficiency exists at the intersection of behavioral finance and market microstructure, and systematic exploitation of those inefficiencies — at scale, with discipline — produces risk-adjusted returns structurally uncorrelated to traditional beta.

Our signal architecture draws on three independent alpha streams: cross-sectional momentum with volatility scaling, macro regime detection through yield curve topology, and options flow imbalance as an institutional sentiment proxy. Each signal is orthogonal by design. Combined, they produce a portfolio engine that remains coherent across market regimes.

Risk management is not a constraint on the strategy — it is the strategy. Position sizing derives from realized covariance. Target volatility is fixed at 6–8% annualized. All drawdown triggers are hard-coded in execution logic rather than operator discretion. The result is a return profile that is positively skewed, right-tailed, and structurally uncorrelated to public market beta.
