# QNT30442 — Capital Intelligence Engine

Integrated a manual-approval capital intelligence layer into Quantora.

## Included
- Strategy scorecard engine
- Capital efficiency metrics
- Ranking and leaderboard generation
- Rebalance recommendation engine
- Approval and rejection action logging
- Capital reallocation apply path
- Dedicated Capital Intelligence panel

## Manual control mode
Recommendations are generated automatically, but capital only moves when an authenticated workspace admin approves a recommendation.
