# QNT30451 — Portfolio Risk Nerve Center

## Mission objective
Install a central portfolio risk command layer across books, limits, alerts, and stress scenarios.

## Added backend capabilities
- exposure registry
- risk limit registry
- alert registry
- stress scenario registry
- risk summary package and health score

## Added frontend capability
- portfolio risk nerve center panel

## Key endpoints
- `GET /workspace/risk/summary`
- `POST /workspace/risk/exposures/create`
- `POST /workspace/risk/limits/create`
- `POST /workspace/risk/alerts/create`
- `POST /workspace/risk/stress/create`

## Strategic value
This mission creates the central risk nerve center required for multi-strategy, multi-broker, institutional capital protection.
