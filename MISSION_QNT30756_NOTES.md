# QNT30756 — Regulatory Obligation Calendar and Deadline Control Layer

Integrated institutional deadline discipline into Quantora's regulatory stack.

## Added
- obligation calendar scheduling
- deadline event registration
- control attestation capture
- obligation completion tracking
- deadline posture scoring linked to regulator readiness, compliance automation, multi-jurisdiction governance, supervisory examination, and filing submission orchestration

## Objective
Provide an institution-grade control layer for monitoring recurring regulatory obligations, preserving evidence continuity, and preventing deadline breaches before capital or supervisory impact occurs.
