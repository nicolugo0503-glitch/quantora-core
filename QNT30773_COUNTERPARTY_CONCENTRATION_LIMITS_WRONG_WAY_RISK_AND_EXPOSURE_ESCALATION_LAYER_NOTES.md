# QNT30773 — Regulatory Counterparty Concentration Limits, Wrong-Way Risk, and Exposure Escalation Layer

This mission institutionalizes concentration-limit discipline across prime broker, counterparty, and collateral relationships.

## Control objectives
- govern concentration diversification against limit policy
- monitor wrong-way risk control and hedge effectiveness
- track exposure headroom before breach formation
- escalate unresolved limit and wrong-way conditions into command action

## System bindings
- qnt30772 counterparty safeguarding and collateral protection
- qnt30759 capital adequacy surveillance
- qnt30760 liquidity stress command
