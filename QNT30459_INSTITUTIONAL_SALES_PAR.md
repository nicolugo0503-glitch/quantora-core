# QNT30459 — Institutional Sales + Partnership Engine

## Mission objective
Install the institutional sales and partnerships operating layer.

## Added backend capabilities
- enterprise lead registry
- opportunity pipeline registry
- partnership registry
- partner activity registry
- sales and partnership summary package and health score

## Added frontend capability
- institutional sales and partnership panel

## Key endpoints
- `GET /workspace/sales-partnerships/summary`
- `POST /workspace/sales-partnerships/leads/create`
- `POST /workspace/sales-partnerships/opps/create`
- `POST /workspace/sales-partnerships/partners/create`
- `POST /workspace/sales-partnerships/activities/create`

## Strategic value
This mission gives Quantora a formal institutional distribution engine across enterprise sales and partnerships.
